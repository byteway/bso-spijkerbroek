### Introduction – BSO Jeans Plugin

What if learning economics was not just about theory, but about running your own company?

The **BSO Jeans plugin** brings the classic jeans simulation game to life in a modern, digital environment. Where the game once depended on Excel sheets, error-prone formulas, and manual calculations, this plugin provides a single, reliable platform where everything works, offers real-time insights, and is instantly playable.

Students take on the role of suppliers and work together to make decisions about production, pricing, and marketing. After each round, they immediately see the impact of their choices and can adjust their strategy accordingly. This transforms the game from a one-off simulation into a consistent and engaging learning experience centered around data, collaboration, and strategy.

The strength of this plugin lies in its simplicity and accessibility: participants can register themselves, submit their decisions online, and track their progress without relying on intermediaries. This creates an interactive learning environment where theory, practice, and gameplay come together.

The BSO Jeans plugin turns a powerful but logistically challenging game into a scalable, reliable, and future-proof digital experience.
